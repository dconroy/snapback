Always remembering the last 30 seconds

Instant replay for anything with HDMI.

Snapback is a tiny Raspberry Pi box that quietly buffers whatever's on your HDMI source. Something great happens? Tap one button on your phone and keep the last 30 seconds, or grab a full-res screenshot.

Two buttons. That's the whole app.

No accounts, no settings screens, no app to install. Open a web page on your phone and tap.

1

Plug it in

HDMI from your console, camera, or computer goes into a Cam Link on a Raspberry Pi 5.

2

It keeps rolling

Snapback encodes a continuous H.264 buffer and keeps about the last minute on the Pi.

3

Tap to keep it

Open the Pi's web page on your phone. Save a screenshot or the last 30 seconds straight to it.

Small on purpose.

A home project that does one job well and stays out of the way.

Rolling 30-second buffer

Saved clips are stitched without re-encoding, so they're ready in about a second. If the HDMI drops, the buffer restarts on its own.

1080p60 in, full-res out

Screenshots are full 1920x1080 JPEGs. Clips are H.264 + AAC MP4s that play anywhere.

Made for your phone

A single mobile web page with big, thumb-sized buttons, that works in your phone's browser.

Local network only

Your captures live on the Pi. No cloud, no telemetry, no accounts, nothing phoning home.

Easy to hack on

Python, FastAPI, ffmpeg, and one HTML file. No Docker, no database, no JS build step.

Watch it live

See the HDMI feed on your phone as it happens, or tap for full live video with sound.

What you need

Off-the-shelf parts, no soldering, no GPIO.

HDMI source→Cam Link 4K→ USB →Raspberry Pi 5→ Wi-Fi →Your phone
  • Raspberry Pi 5 4 GB is plenty
  • Elgato Cam Link 4K USB HDMI capture
  • microSD card 32 GB+, a good one
  • Official Pi 5 power supply 27 W USB-C
  • HDMI splitter Optional: keep watching on a TV
  • Active cooler Recommended: encoding runs nonstop

Set it up in a few minutes

On Raspberry Pi OS with ffmpeg and Python 3 installed. Full notes are in the README.

# on the Pi
git clone https://github.com/dconroy/snapback.git ~/snapback
cd ~/snapback
python3 -m venv .venv
.venv/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt
.venv/bin/python -m snapback serve

# on your phone
open http://<your-pi>.local:8080/
